Where to find the numbers

Layouts differ between auction houses, but both numbers are labelled on every sheet.

出品番号 · LOT NO.00000
オークション · AUCTIONNAME / DATE
車台番号 · CHASSIS NO.XXXXX-0000000
評価 · GRADE0.0
走行 · MILEAGE00,000 km
Lot number 出品番号: usually near the top, beside the auction name and date.
Chassis number 車台番号: in the vehicle details block. It is the model code, a hyphen, then the serial number.

How it works

  1. Enter the numbers

    Add the chassis number, lot number and your WhatsApp number.

  2. Send the sheet

    WhatsApp opens with your details filled in. Attach a photo or PDF of the full sheet and send.

  3. We check with Japan

    We compare your sheet with the original auction record and reply on WhatsApp with what we find.

For a good photo: send the whole sheet, not a crop. Keep all four edges in view and the text sharp enough to read. If the sheet has several pages, send every page.
