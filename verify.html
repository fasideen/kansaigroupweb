What is a Japanese auction sheet?

A Japanese auction sheet is the condition report an auction house inspector prepares for each car before it is sold. It usually shows the overall grade (for example 4.5, or R and RA for a repaired car), an interior grade, the mileage at the time of inspection, a diagram marking dents, scratches and repairs, and the inspector's notes.

For recon and imported cars in Malaysia, the sheet is the main record of the car's condition when it left Japan.

Why verify an auction sheet before you buy?

A paper or photo copy of a sheet does not prove it belongs to the car in front of you. Sheets can be copied, edited or attached to a different car.

Comparing the chassis and auction details with available records can help identify inconsistencies. A document check does not replace an independent inspection of the actual car.

What it does not guarantee

An auction sheet describes the vehicle at the time of auction in Japan. It cannot establish the car’s current mechanical condition, whether it was damaged later, or whether the seller has disclosed everything.

Always inspect the actual vehicle before buying.

Auction sheet verification: common questions

How do I verify a Japan auction sheet in Malaysia?

Fill in the form on this page with the chassis number, lot number and auction date from the sheet. WhatsApp then opens with your details filled in. Attach a photo or PDF of the sheet and send it. We review the details and reply on WhatsApp with what can be established.

What do I need to verify an auction sheet?

You need the chassis number, the lot number and the auction date from the sheet, plus a clear photo or PDF of the whole sheet. If you know which auction house it came from, add that too.

How old can the auction sheet be?

We can only verify auction sheets from the last 5 years, counted from the auction date printed on the sheet. We cannot verify older sheets.

Where do I find the chassis number and lot number on an auction sheet?

The lot number (出品番号) is usually near the top of the sheet, beside the auction name and date. The chassis number (車台番号) is in the vehicle details block. It is the model code, a hyphen, then the serial number.

Can I verify an auction sheet before I pay for the car?

Yes. The check only needs the sheet, so you can do it before you pay or while you are still deciding.
