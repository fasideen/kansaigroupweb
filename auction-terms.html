AUCTION TERMS

Last updated 28 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-28-offer-1

1. Who we are

These terms apply to online auctions run by Kansai Group Malaysia Sdn. Bhd. ("Kansai Group", "we", "us") on kansaigroup.my. By sending an offer or placing a bid you agree to them.

2. Making an offer

You must be at least 18. To make an offer, enter the amount, your real name and a mobile number you can be reached on by WhatsApp, then send the prepared WhatsApp message to our team. This registers you as a bidder. Your offer is recorded straight away but is not shown publicly until our team has verified you. We may verify, refuse or block any bidder at our discretion, and we may decline offers from bidders we cannot verify.

Once you are verified we send you a bidder code. Offers placed with your code go live straight away. Your bidder code is personal to you. Keep it private. You are responsible for bids placed with your code.

3. Refundable deposit

A refundable deposit is required before we verify you and your offers go live. After you send your offer, our team will confirm the deposit amount, payment instructions and refund conditions in the WhatsApp chat. Send your payment receipt there; sending an offer alone does not confirm payment. We verify payment before approving you and sending a bidder code. No payment is taken on the website. How the deposit is applied to a purchase, refunded, or treated if a winning bidder does not complete the purchase will be confirmed to you in writing when the deposit is taken.

4. How bidding works

  • Each auction shows its own start price, current bid and end time. Where a closing time is set, offers close at that time unless we update the listing. Some stock listings remain open without a deadline. Refresh the page to see the latest listing details.
  • Each new offer must be at least RM 500 above the current highest verified offer or starting price, and must be a whole multiple of RM 500. The page shows the next valid minimum amount. The current highest offer shown on the page includes verified offers only.
  • An offer (or bid) is an offer to purchase. It cannot be withdrawn by the bidder once sent. It is not binding on Kansai Group until we confirm it. An offer from a bidder we have not verified by the time the listing closes may be declined.
  • The highest valid offer when the listing closes is the leading offer only. It does not automatically create a sale or a right to purchase. Kansai Group may accept or decline any offer. If we accept an offer, we will contact that customer on WhatsApp to confirm the sale and arrange payment within the period we state at that time.
  • We may remove an offer that is fake, made in error, made by a blocked or unverified bidder, or that breaks these terms. We keep a record of removed offers.

5. Price

Vehicles are located in Malaysia. Unless the listing says otherwise, the price already includes import duty and AP. Any additional charges (for example an admin fee or ownership transfer) are shown on the auction page and confirmed before purchase.

6. Vehicle information

We describe each vehicle in good faith, based on the information we hold, including Japanese auction grade sheets. Please ask us for more detail or arrange a viewing before you rely on the description. Photos are for illustration.

7. Conduct

Do not place fake offers, offer to push up the price without intending to buy, use another person's name, number or code, or try to interfere with the website. We may block bidders who do and may keep or apply deposits as confirmed in writing at the deposit stage.

To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for losses caused by website downtime, internet problems or events outside our control. Nothing here limits rights you have under Malaysian law that cannot be limited.
