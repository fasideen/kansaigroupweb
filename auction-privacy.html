PRIVACY NOTICE

Last updated 28 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Kansai Group Malaysia Sdn. Bhd. (SSM 202601018333) is responsible for the personal data described here, under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

2. What we collect

  • When you make an offer: your name, mobile number, the offer amount and time, a reference, the listing you made the offer on, the date you agreed to our terms, and your deposit and verification status. When you continue on WhatsApp, you choose to send a prepared message with these details and the listing link. Deposit discussions and any receipt you send take place in that chat.
  • Who sees your offer: only our staff can see your name and number. Until we verify you, your offer is visible to our staff only. Once you are verified, the public sees the amount and a masked name such as "Ahm***".
  • When you browse: an anonymous visitor ID stored in your browser, your device type (phone or computer), the website you came from, and which pages and auctions you viewed. We use Google Analytics and a counter of our own for this.

3. Why we use it

To approve bidders, run and record auctions, contact winning bidders, prevent fake bids and fraud, handle deposits, keep records, and understand how many people visit and bid so we can improve the service.

4. Who sees it

Our staff see your name and number. We use service providers to run the website and its database (Supabase) and to measure visits (Google). WhatsApp is our main channel for deposit and approval communication. If staff alerts are enabled, we also use Telegram to notify our team of registrations and offers, including the customer name and, for registrations, phone number. These providers may process data on servers outside Malaysia. We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose it where the law requires.

5. Your browser

We store your name, mobile number, the offers you sent from this device, your bidder code (if you have one) and a visitor ID in your browser (local storage), so you do not have to enter them every time. On a shared device, use "Change" or "Not you?" in the offer window to clear them, or clear your browser data.

6. How long we keep it

We keep registration and bid records for as long as needed for auction, accounting and legal purposes, then delete or anonymise them.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or withdraw your consent. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer let you bid. Contact us using the details below and we will respond as the PDPA requires.

8. Security

Bidder details are stored in access-controlled systems that only authorised staff can read. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us on WhatsApp or by email using the details below. See also our Auction Terms.
